# Research Experience

This directory collects research reports, slide decks, and presentation materials from Jerome Y. Wu's undergraduate research experiences.

## Overview

These materials reflect my research experience in structural biology, protein biochemistry, crystallography, molecular biology, and related areas. They include final presentations and research proposals from undergraduate research programs and internships.

## Contents

- [SULI 2025](SULI%202025/)

Final report, slide deck, IGNITE slide deck, and IGNITE presentation video from the DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI) program at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, conducted in collaboration with Stanford University’s Wakatsuki lab.

Topics include crystallization screening, X-ray crystallography, SAXS experimental design, fluorescent proteins, spectral characterization, and AlphaFold-based structural exploration.

- [SULI 2024](SULI%202024/)

Final report and slide deck from the DOE Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI) program at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, conducted in collaboration with Stanford University’s Wakatsuki lab.

Topics include recombinant protein expression, protein purification, and interdisciplinary structural biology research.

- [Genomics Research Internship Program at Stanford (GRIPS) 2023](GRIPS%202023/)

Final presentation from my 2023 Genomics Research Internship Program at Stanford (GRIPS) research experience. 

Topics include mutagenesis, transfection, experimental troubleshooting, shRNA-based gene knockdown, and molecular biology approaches to viral co-infection research.
