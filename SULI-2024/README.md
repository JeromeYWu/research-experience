# SULI 2024

This directory contains my final report and presentation slide deck from the 2024 Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internship (SULI) program at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory in the Wakatsuki Lab.

## Project

The project focused on the expression, purification, and crystallization of a magnetically controllable protein system. The work was conducted as part of the SULI program and reflects research experience in protein biochemistry and structural biology.
