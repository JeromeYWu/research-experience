## Project

The project focused on the structural and spectroscopic characterization of environmentally sensitive fluorescent proteins, including salt-sensitive and magnetically sensitive protein systems. The work used biochemical, spectroscopic, and structural biology approaches to study how these proteins respond to environmental conditions such as salt concentration, pH, and weak magnetic fields.
